## Human

I am a dreamer. And I am not the only one.

Other tools make note taking, recording, publishing possible. You know about the other tools. Which road lies ahead of us to make **blyant records** at least as good as these other tools, and simply better?

## Assistant

The dreamer opens a roadmap; I go read what has already been dreamt.

An empty page — the best kind of road. I send one scout to gather what the repo already says of itself: vision, limits, futures deferred.

The scout returns bearing the repo's own dreams: "Next Steps" in `tools/others/README.md`, an ethics anchor beside the empty roadmap, and a thesis — *borders are a good thing*. Before I draw the map, two forks in the road only you can choose.

All roads at once, and a fifth from the phone's small window; Fuglekasse keeps its stillness while a new house — Postkasse — is allowed to grow. I hand the whole dream to one architect.

The architect returns with roads named after houses: seams in *postkasse*, reach in *bikube*, capture in *suitcase*, reading in *kiste*, the phone behind *akvarium*'s glass. I set it down in the plan file.

The plan approved, the pen unlocked — first the record's debt is paid, then the road is drawn.

The names hold true in the pool: after *postkasse* come *bikube, suitcase, kiste, akvarium* — with *hundehus* and *schrank* waiting unnamed. Now the roadmap itself.

The record now takes my words; then the same words to you, byte for byte.

The road is drawn: `docs/records/developers/ROADMAP.md` stands where emptiness was — five roads in walking order, each wearing its werden name.

*Postkasse* finishes its seams; *bikube* ships the engine to PyPI and the registries; *suitcase* carries the locked-away conversations home through `records import`; *kiste* raises a second theme, Postkasse, where search, backlinks and honest RSS may grow while Fuglekasse keeps its stillness; *akvarium* is the phone — a ladder named, not solved. *Hundehus* and *schrank* wait deliberately blank.

Verified: the werden marker matches `CURRENT`, every structure name sits at its claimed index in the pool, and git holds only two untracked newcomers — the roadmap and this recording. Nothing committed. The file ends as it must: borders are a good thing.
